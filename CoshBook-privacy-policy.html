Cosh Book Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Cosh Book is a private, offline-first expense tracker for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your data stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not have servers, and we never see your data.

Data the app stores

  • The transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, notes, tags and receipt photos you enter.
  • Your app settings, such as currency, theme and language.
  • A PIN, if you set one. It is stored only as a salted hash on your phone.

All of this is kept in an encrypted database on your device. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.

Google sign-in and Drive backup (optional)

You can use Cosh Book without a Google account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets it save encrypted backups in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see or change any other files in your Drive. Backups are encrypted with AES-256 before they leave your phone.

Google's handling of your account and Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No servers of our own: nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only for Google Drive backup and restore.
  • Notifications: budget alerts and recurring-transaction reminders.
  • Biometric: optional app lock.
  • Receipt photos are chosen through the Android photo picker, so the app does not need camera or storage permission.

Files you export

Excel, PDF and CSV exports and local backup files are saved only where you choose, or shared only with the app you pick. What happens to them afterwards is up to you.

How to delete your data

  • Everything on your phone: in the app, go to Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Drive backups: go to More → Sign out and choose to delete Drive backups. Or, on the web, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Cosh Book → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke Drive access: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Cosh Book.

Children

Cosh Book is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
